# Customer Support FAQ (Knowledge Base)

This document is the grounded knowledge base the Copilot Studio agent uses to deflect common questions. When a customer message matches one of these topics, the agent answers from here and cites this source instead of raising a case.

Topics covered: Billing, Account and login, Subscription, Contacting support.

---

## Billing

**What payment methods do you accept?**
We accept major credit and debit cards. Payment is taken automatically on your billing date.

**I was charged twice, what do I do?**
Duplicate charges usually clear on their own within a few working days. If the second charge is still showing after that, raise a case and our team will investigate and refund any genuine duplicate.

**When am I billed?**
You are billed on the same date each month, based on the date you first subscribed. You can see your next billing date in your account settings.

**How do I get an invoice or receipt?**
Receipts are emailed automatically after each payment. You can also download past receipts from the billing section of your account.

## Account and login

**How do I reset my password?**
1. Go to the sign-in page.
2. Click "Forgot password".
3. Check your email for a password reset link.
4. Click the link and follow the instructions to create a new password.
The password reset link is valid for 30 minutes. If you do not receive the email or the link does not work, raise a case and we will help.

**How do I change my email address?**
Open your account settings, go to the profile section, and update your email address. You will need to confirm the change from a link sent to the new address.

**Why am I locked out of my account?**
Accounts are temporarily locked after several failed sign-in attempts. Wait 15 minutes and try again, or reset your password using the steps above.

## Subscription

**How do I upgrade or downgrade my plan?**
Open your account settings, go to the subscription section, and choose a different plan. Changes take effect from your next billing date.

**How do I cancel my subscription?**
Go to the subscription section of your account settings and select cancel. Your plan stays active until the end of the current billing period.

**Will I be refunded if I cancel?**
Cancelling stops future payments. The current period is not refunded, but you keep access until it ends.

## Contacting support

**How do I reach a human agent?**
Describe your issue to the assistant. If it is something that needs a person, the assistant will log a support case and the team will follow up by email.

**What are your support hours?**
Cases are reviewed during standard business hours, Monday to Friday. High priority issues such as suspected account compromise are flagged for faster handling.
